Heavy-Duty Mounting Bracket Assembly

A fully constrained multi-part assembly that holds a cross-support shaft between two vertical brackets and bolts down to a base plate. The design intent was a mount that is secure, adjustable and buildable from standard hardware.

Parts list

ItemPartQty
1Base plate with patterned mounting holes1
2L-shaped vertical support bracket (mirrored pair)2
3Cross-support shaft1
4ANSI hex bolts and washers (SolidWorks Toolbox)4

Process

  1. Part geometry. Built each part with Boss-Extrude, then added bores and fastener holes with Extruded Cut and Hole Wizard. Fillets and chamfers break sharp edges and relieve the bracket's inside corner.
  2. Patterns and symmetry. Used Linear Pattern for repeated hole layouts and Mirror for the opposite bracket, so one design drives both sides.
  3. Mates. Coincident mates seat the brackets on the plate, Concentric mates align the shaft and bolts with their holes, and Distance mates set bracket spacing. The assembly is fully constrained.
  4. Hardware. Seated standard ANSI fasteners from the Toolbox library so the design uses off-the-shelf parts.
Design intentSecure, adjustable bracket mounting
ConstraintFully defined with Coincident, Concentric and Distance mates
Real-world useSame workflow as chassis brackets and equipment mounts
